Our project is Attune — in Chinese, Zhi Yin, “the shade that knows”. It is a shade for the weekend pedestrian street on Daxue Road. It senses the sun and heat, reads what the people below are doing, and adjusts its leaves one by one. The 3D model on the right is the real-time version of our Grasshopper model.　我们的项目叫“知荫”——知人的阴影。它放在大学路周末步行街上，感知太阳，读懂下面的人，再一片片调整叶子。

Before showing how the device works, let's look at who is under it. These figures come from our own street section. An older man needs deep, steady shade and a seat. A young woman looking at her phone needs compact shade over the screen. A parent with a small child needs shade at two heights and an open view to watch the child. Friends passing through stay briefly and want to talk. One fixed canopy can only serve one of them — that is why the shade has to adapt.　先看看装置下面是谁：老人需要稳定的深荫和座位；玩手机的年轻人需要罩住屏幕；带孩子的家长需要覆盖两种身高、保留看护视线；结伴路过的人短暂停留、需要交谈空间。固定的遮阳只能服务一种人，所以遮阳必须可变。

So what is Attune? It is a canopy of rotating leaves. The leaves are translucent: overlapped they give deep shade, spread out they let in soft, diffuse light. The hollow column works as a chimney — warm air rises and leaves through the top, drawing cooler air in at the seat, so the bench stays cool. And because the lower leaves lift, tilt and fold, the canopy also works as a spatial boundary: open for a conversation, closed around someone sitting alone.　知荫是一顶由可旋转叶片组成的遮阳：叶片半透明，叠合时阴影浓、散开时光线柔；中筒有烟囱效应，保证座椅始终凉爽；下层叶片能升降翻折，为不同的社交行为形成不同的空间边界。

CONTROL VARIABLE 02 · P
A vision model
labels each person
大模型为每个人打标签
The second control variable is the person, scored as P. A vision-language model looks at each person the camera sees and fills in seven labels: apparent age, top, bottom, carried items, companionship, posture and social state. These are real detections on Daxue Road. The first five labels feed the P score — who needs shade most; posture and social state go to the behaviour rules. When the model fails, as here, it says so instead of guessing.　第二个控制变量是人（P）。大模型为相机看到的每个人填七个标签：年龄段、上装、下装、随行物、结伴关系、姿态、社交状态。前五项进入 P 评分，决定谁更需要遮阳；姿态和社交状态交给行为规则。识别失败时直接标记失败，而不是猜。

Behaviour · ZED skeleton 行为骨骼
This slide carries the whole logic. The four buttons are four scenes; each mainly triggers one evaluation in the loop. P1: a second person arrives and two people talk — density rises, the upper leaves gather and overlap. C2: one sits, one stands — each leaf lifts or lowers for the person beneath it. C3: head down at a phone — the leaf tilts forward and folds down, and opens again when the person looks up. C4: someone rests while a passer-by walks through — the steady sitter is enclosed, the passer-by is ignored. People move, the field changes, the leaves follow, and the new shade changes how people behave — that is the loop.　四个场景各触发一个评价维度：人一动，灰阶就变，叶片跟着变，阴影和开口又改变人的行为——这就是循环。

After moving, the unit looks again every 0.35 s — but it must not follow every head turn. Five time rules: hold for half a second, ignore changes under 20 mm and 2°, limit speeds, slow down when stable, and clear history when the person changes.　五条时间规则：持续半秒、死区、限速、随稳定度变慢、换人清空。

This is the loop we implemented, one frame every 350 ms. Sense: the street radiation base and the ZED skeleton; the vision-language model interface is ready. Decide: a weighted source, four ground fields, P1–C4 rule decoding. Act: upper rotation, an 18-channel time filter, real leaf geometry in Grasshopper. The movement changes shade and posture, and the next frame looks again.　感知、判断、行动、再感知，每 350 毫秒一圈：整街辐射与骨骼 → 加权灰阶与 P1–C4 解码 → 上层旋转、时间滤波、叶片几何 → 遮阳与姿态改变，进入下一帧。
